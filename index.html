Our Journey

A Fermentation
Like No Other

Wonder Coffee has been cultivating specialty coffee in the highlands of Chiang Rai since 2009. Our Cartimor variety grows at 1,200 metres above sea level — and we process it in a way no one else does.

We adapted SCOBY — the living culture of bacteria and yeast famous for fermenting kombucha — to work on coffee cherries. The result transforms Cartimor's natural character into something extraordinary.

01
Cartimor Cherry Harvest
Hand-picked at peak ripeness in the Chiang Rai highlands at 1,200 m.a.s.l.
02
SCOBY Inoculation
A carefully selected Symbiotic Culture of Bacteria and Yeast is introduced to the coffee cherries.
03
Controlled Fermentation
Natural sugars transform at 20–30°C, developing fruity complexity and bright acidity.
04
Light Roast
Roasted light to preserve every nuance of the fermentation-developed flavour profile.
